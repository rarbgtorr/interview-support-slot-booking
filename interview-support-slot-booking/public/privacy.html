Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Information you provide

When you book a demo or interview support appointment, we collect your name, email address, WhatsApp number, interviewing company name, overall experience, interview stage, timezone, notes, and the documents or job description you provide.

How we use your information

We use these details to arrange and manage your appointments and provide interview support. Your booking email lets you sign in using an email verification code to view, reschedule, cancel, or update the WhatsApp number and notes on your upcoming appointments.

Access and notifications

The public calendar displays availability without other members’ booking details. Administrators can access booking information and available documents. Enabled administrator phones receive encrypted notifications containing the booking type, name and appointment time. Email is used for login verification codes.

Storage and retention

The website is hosted on Netlify, and Supabase provides database, authentication and private document storage. Chrome’s push service delivers administrator notifications. Uploaded files become unavailable 24 hours after the final appointment associated with those files ends. A background task deletes expired uploads; failed deletions are retried. Rescheduling updates this deadline. Appointment records, notes and pasted job description text are retained separately for booking management. Notification delivery records are cleaned up after seven days.

Browser storage and permissions

Login sessions use secure, HttpOnly cookies. Your selected display timezone may be saved in your browser. Administrator notifications require permission on each phone and can be disabled from the admin page or browser settings.

Questions and removal requests

For questions about your information, or to request correction or removal, contact Wingfactor IT Support through the contact channel you use to arrange your support sessions.
