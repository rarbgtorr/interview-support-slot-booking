Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Information you provide

When you book a demo or interview support appointment, we collect your name, email address, WhatsApp number, interviewing company name, overall experience, interview stage, timezone, notes, and the documents or job description you provide.

How we use your information

We keep your information confidential and use these details to arrange and manage your appointments and feed our modules to provide accurate answers tailored to your profile during interview support. Your booking email lets you sign in using an email verification code to view, reschedule, cancel, or update the WhatsApp number and notes on your upcoming appointments.

Storage and retention

Uploaded files will get automatically deleted 24 hours after the appointment ends.

Browser storage and permissions

Login sessions use secure, HttpOnly cookies. Your selected display timezone may be saved in your browser.

Questions and removal requests

For questions about your information, or to request correction or removal, contact Wingfactor IT Support through the contact channel you use to arrange your support sessions.
